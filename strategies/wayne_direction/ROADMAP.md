## Final stop ruling

The frozen stop rule was triggered because the sequence remained below the independent sample gates.

Prohibited rescue paths:

- loosening technical definitions;
- replacing the conservative endpoint with the favorable stretch endpoint;
- post-outcome pair shopping;
- consuming the 2022-2025 holdout;
- adding yield, VIX, macro, seasonality or extra indicators;
- converting descriptive reach into strategy P&L.

A future study would require a genuinely new preregistered cohort, materially more history or a separately justified asset family. It would be a new programme and must compete against higher-frequency strategy research for resources.

## Recommended resource allocation

Redirect research effort toward strategies that:

- generate materially more independent opportunities;
- have a direct executable entry and invalidation path;
- can support robust pair/year breadth without conditioning away the sample;
- can be tested with BID/ASK costs before extensive macro engineering.
