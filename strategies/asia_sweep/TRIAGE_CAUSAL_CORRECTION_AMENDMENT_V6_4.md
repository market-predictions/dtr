# Asian Sweep Triage Causal-Correction Amendment v6.4

Date frozen: 2026-07-26  
Applies to v6.0–v6.3.

## 1. Status correction

The development-only triage metrics quoted in v6.1 were generated by a local prototype and are **withdrawn before any authoritative source-backed workflow or protected validation**.

Audit found that the prototype constructed group-held-out probabilities for the resolvability component, but used a direction model fitted on the complete outer-training population to score unresolved outer-training rows during threshold selection. Direction labels exist only on resolved rows; scoring the unresolved threshold-selection rows with the full fitted model introduced in-sample information into the selective-action threshold procedure.

The defect did not access 2020–2025 and did not affect any protected outcome. It invalidates the provisional Phase-A metrics, even though those metrics had produced a failure.

Therefore:

- the numerical Phase-A findings in v6.1 are non-authoritative and must not be cited as the programme result;
- `FAIL_TRIAGE_STOP_BEFORE_PROTECTED_VALIDATION` is not yet the authoritative decision;
- staged and extended studies remain blocked from source-backed execution until corrected triage tests pass;
- all gates, labels, thresholds and partitions remain unchanged.

## 2. Corrected causal construction

For each inner Amsterdam-week fold:

1. fit the resolvability model on the fold's fitting weeks and predict every row in the held-out weeks;
2. fit the reversal-versus-continuation model only on resolved rows from the fitting weeks;
3. use that direction model to predict **every** row in the held-out weeks, including unresolved rows needed for action-threshold selection;
4. score direction hyperparameters only on resolved rows in the held-out weeks;
5. fit the direction calibrator only on resolved group-held-out predictions;
6. apply the fitted calibrator to all group-held-out direction predictions;
7. select reversal/continuation/abstain thresholds solely from the resulting fully group-held-out probabilities.

The same construction is repeated independently inside every outer leave-one-year-out fold.

## 3. Operational response

The corrected implementation must pass synthetic checks proving that:

- changing labels in a threshold-validation week cannot alter probabilities for that same week through model fitting;
- unresolved rows receive direction probabilities from a model that never fitted their week;
- future-appended rows do not alter prior fold probabilities or actions;
- threshold selection is deterministic under stable row ordering.

Only the corrected authoritative development workflow may issue a Phase-A decision.
