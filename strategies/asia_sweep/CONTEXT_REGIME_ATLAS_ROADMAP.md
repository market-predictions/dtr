# Asian Sweep Context and Regime Atlas — Roadmap

## Current programme

The programme evaluates causal higher-timeframe direction, trend strength, trend change, volatility regime, causal range location and limited session/calendar diagnostics around the validated T5 Asian Sweep reversal fingerprint.

## Phase A — Single-factor attribution

Status: `IMPLEMENTED_PENDING_AUTHORITATIVE_RUN`

- reconstruct EURUSD and GBPUSD 2015–2021 T5 populations;
- attach strictly pre-event D1/W1 and location context;
- calculate the frozen stressed payoff proxy;
- evaluate every preregistered state independently;
- apply pair/year breadth, concentration, block inference and FDR gates.

Decision:

- fewer than two independent passing families: stop before interactions;
- at least two independent passing families: freeze at most six interactions.

## Phase B — Two-factor interactions

Status: `BLOCKED_BY_PHASE_A`

- mechanically choose non-redundant cross-family pairs;
- freeze pair definitions before interaction outcomes;
- no three-way interactions or threshold search;
- repeat economic, breadth, concentration and FDR gates.

## Phase C — Adaptive regime router

Status: `BLOCKED_BY_PHASE_B`

- opened only after broad two-factor evidence;
- action remains reversal participation versus abstention;
- no entry, stop, target or position-management changes;
- grouped out-of-fold evaluation only.

## Phase D — Replication

Status: `FUTURE_DATA_REQUIRED`

A surviving candidate must be replicated unchanged on genuinely fresh future EURUSD/GBPUSD observations or separately qualified cross-asset data. Existing 2022–2025 fingerprint holdouts cannot be relabelled as untouched regime validation.

## Deployment boundary

No Pine, alerts, sizing, paper trading or deployment is authorized until replication and executable strategy validation both pass.
