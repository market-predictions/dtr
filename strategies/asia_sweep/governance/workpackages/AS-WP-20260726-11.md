## Root cause

The validated T5 model continued to rank midpoint completion, but the strongest confirmation scores generally arrived after a substantial portion of the reversal had already occurred. Remaining target distance contracted while entry-to-stop risk did not contract proportionately. Consequently, the highest model-score quintile had the worst market-entry expectancy.

The two preregistered limit challengers did not restore stable expectancy.
