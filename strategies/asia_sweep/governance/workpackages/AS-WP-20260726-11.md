## Causal event-time policy

The retrospective weekly Hit@1 ranking is diagnostic only and is not executable. Events are processed chronologically.

Primary live-selection rule:

1. the event is T5 eligible and unresolved;
2. its frozen probability is at or above the threshold;
3. no position is open in that pair;
4. no earlier trade has been taken in that pair on the same Amsterdam date;
5. entry remains possible before 10:00 Amsterdam.

Cross-pair simultaneous positions are retained for per-trade research and handled separately by the portfolio-risk layer.

## Frozen execution family

All variants share:

- target: Asian midpoint;
- stop: the original adverse-continuation barrier, `0.20 × Asian range` beyond the sweep extreme;
- time exit: close of the final active M1 bar before 10:00 Amsterdam;
- bid/ask execution from the qualified Dukascopy source;
- conservative stop-first ordering when stop and target are touched inside the same M1 bar;
- one trade maximum per pair per Amsterdam date;
- no entry when the executable price has already crossed the target or stop;
- no overnight position.

Preregistered entry variants:

1. `MKT_NEXT_OPEN` — market at the first active minute open strictly after T5;
2. `LIMIT_ASIAN_BOUNDARY` — post-T5 limit at the swept Asian boundary, valid until 10:00;
3. `LIMIT_HALF_RETRACE` — post-T5 limit halfway between the T5 midpoint close and the swept Asian boundary, valid until 10:00.

The primary baseline is `MKT_NEXT_OPEN`. Challengers may replace it only through the frozen development hierarchy.

## Cost and fill rules

- spread is embedded through executable BID/ASK bars;
- target limit fills at the target price when the executable side touches it;
- market entry and time exit use the executable side open/close;
- stop fills at the stop price in base results;
- additional slippage stresses: `0.10` pip and `0.25` pip per market/stop execution;
- missing or inactive quotes invalidate the affected order minute rather than being forward-filled.

## Risk normalization

Initial risk is the executable entry-to-stop distance after side selection. Trades with non-positive risk or non-positive target distance are invalid.

Each trade is reported in R:

- target exit: executable price return divided by initial risk;
- stop exit: approximately `-1R` before incremental slippage;
- time exit: marked to the executable side at 10:00;
- no position sizing or compounding authority at this stage.

5. Ties are resolved by lower maximum drawdown, then more trades, then simpler execution in this order: market, boundary limit, half-retrace limit.

## Discovery gates

A candidate must satisfy all of the following on 2015–2019:

- at least `150` filled trades pooled;
- at least `50` filled trades per pair;
- net expectancy greater than `+0.05R` per trade;
- median annual expectancy greater than zero;
- positive expectancy in at least `4 / 5` years;
- positive expectancy on both EURUSD and GBPUSD;
- maximum drawdown below `20R`;
- return/max-drawdown at least `1.50`;
- one-sided calendar-week block-bootstrap probability expectancy greater than zero at least `95%`;
- expectancy remains positive under `0.10` pip slippage stress.
