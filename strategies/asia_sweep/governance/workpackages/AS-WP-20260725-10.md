## Objective

Test whether a confirmed London rejection of the completed Asian range carries a broad, control-adjusted reversal effect across a fixed ten-pair FX universe. The programme must reject the mechanism before strategy construction when the effect is absent, narrow, concentrated or statistically uncertain.

## Fixed universe

- EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF;
- AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD;
- USDJPY, EURJPY, GBPJPY;
- EURGBP.

Factor blocks are `usd_europe`, `usd_commodity`, `jpy` and `europe_cross`.
