## Objective

Test whether a confirmed London rejection of the completed Asian range carries a broad, control-adjusted reversal effect across a fixed ten-pair FX universe. Reject the mechanism before strategy construction when the effect is absent, narrow, concentrated or statistically uncertain.

## Fixed universe

- EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF;
- AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD;
- USDJPY, EURJPY, GBPJPY;
- EURGBP.

Factor blocks: `usd_europe`, `usd_commodity`, `jpy` and `europe_cross`.

## Final result

- mean event-minus-control effect: `-0.018617` Asian-range fractions;
- 95% date-block interval: `[-0.046224, +0.008483]`;
- permutation p-value: `0.910809`;
- positive pairs: `4 / 10`;
- positive factor blocks: `1 / 4`;
- gates passed: `5 / 10`.

Decision: `FAIL_DISCOVERY_STOP_BEFORE_MECHANISM_VALIDATION`.
