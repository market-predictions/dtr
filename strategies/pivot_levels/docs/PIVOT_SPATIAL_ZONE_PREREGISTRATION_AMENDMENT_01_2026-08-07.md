# Pivot Spatial Zone Preregistration — Amendment 01

Date: 2026-08-07
Programme: `PIVOT-SPATIAL-ZONE-V1`
Status: `PRE_OUTCOME_CLARIFICATION`

## Purpose

The frozen preregistration requires the 5/10/15/20/25% secondary width curve to be “directionally coherent” with any primary 20% broad-zone finding, but did not numerically define coherence.

Before pooled spatial outcomes are calculated, define:

- for S1–S4, width-curve coherence passes only when at least **4 of 5** combined-sample width effects have the same sign as the primary 20% effect;
- the primary 20% development and validation effects must still independently satisfy the original positive-direction gate;
- no secondary width may replace the 20% primary or be selected as a rescue;
- S5 is width-free and therefore has no width-coherence requirement.

No market-data, pivot, trend, barrier, control-zone, sample, holdout or inference definition is changed by this clarification.
