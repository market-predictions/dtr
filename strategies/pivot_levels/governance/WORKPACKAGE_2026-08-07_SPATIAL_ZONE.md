## Interpretation

The broad-zone concern changes the programme conclusion only partially.

Daily and weekly pivots are not shown to be superior magnets or automatic reversal zones. Instead, market occupancy near daily/weekly pivots shows elevated **terminal-extreme hazard**: H1 highs/lows close to a pivot are more likely to become the hindsight-confirmed endpoint of the ATR-defined directional leg than H1 highs/lows farther between pivots.

This is structural evidence and not yet a causal entry rule.
