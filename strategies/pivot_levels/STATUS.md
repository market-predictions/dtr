## Binding current decision

`PROMOTE_DAILY_WEEKLY_PIVOT_TERMINAL_ZONE_TO_HOLDOUT_CONFIRMATION`

The parent five-timeframe classic floor-pivot programme remains valid: exact pivot coordinates did not demonstrate privileged target, stall or reversal behavior versus the original nearby controls, and the stable PP→R3/S3 reach ladder remains descriptive.

The spatial follow-up tested whether those nearby controls were contaminated because the economically relevant object is a broad pivot zone rather than an exact line.

## Spatial follow-up design

- real pivot-zone half-widths: 5/10/15/20/25% of local adjacent-pivot spacing;
- primary broad zone: ±20%;
- far controls: equal-width zones centered halfway between adjacent pivots;
- S1 raw endpoint clustering;
- S2 trend-approach target completion;
- S3 containment/stall allowing neutral sideways dwell;
- S4 reversal before extension;
- S5 occupancy-adjusted terminal-extreme enrichment from pivot center to inter-pivot midpoint;
- 5,000 pair-year-week bootstrap draws;
- Holm familywise correction across 25 primary hypotheses.

## Binding findings

### Not supported

Broad pivot zones do **not** earn a target, containment/stall or reversal advantage over equal-width midpoint controls. S1–S4 produce no positive qualifying mechanism on daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly pivots.

### Supported internally

Daily and weekly S5 terminal-zone enrichment pass the frozen internal gate.

Preregistered close-occupancy metric:

- daily: +4.55 pp combined, development +4.51 pp, validation +4.65 pp, 95% CI +3.74 to +5.33 pp, Holm p < 0.001, 10/10 positive pairs;
- weekly: +1.26 pp combined, development +1.55 pp, validation +0.54 pp, 95% CI +0.60 to +1.94 pp, Holm p 0.008, 9/10 positive pairs.

Adversarial same-geometry robustness using H1 highs for up-leg exposure and H1 lows for down-leg exposure:

- daily: +2.24 pp, 95% CI +1.95 to +2.52 pp, 10/10 positive pairs;
- weekly: +0.63 pp, 95% CI +0.35 to +0.91 pp, 9/10 positive pairs;
- monthly/quarterly/yearly: no qualifying effect.

Daily same-geometry endpoint rates decline monotonically with normalized pivot distance: 32.76%, 31.98%, 31.07%, 30.16%, 30.09% across the 0–10 through 40–50% bins.

Weekly shows the same weaker shape: 31.65%, 31.56%, 31.13%, 30.97%, 30.99%.

## Interpretation boundary

The supported phenomenon is **terminal hazard / turning-point enrichment per market occupancy**, not attraction or guaranteed reversal.

An H1 high/low occurring close to a daily or weekly pivot is modestly more likely to become the hindsight-confirmed terminal extreme of the ATR-defined directional leg than an H1 high/low occurring farther between pivots.

The endpoint is only confirmed after the ATR directional-change threshold. This is not yet a causal reversal-entry signal.
