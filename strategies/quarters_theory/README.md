# Quarters Theory Research

This strategy area tests whether the canonical 250-pip Quarters Theory lattice contains predictive information beyond ordinary 50- and 100-pip round-number behaviour.

It is intentionally separated from DTR and Asia Sweep. Shared loaders and research utilities may be reused, but hypotheses, manifests, results, claims and holdout governance remain independent.

## Current conclusion

`PRICE_ONLY_QUARTER_PROGRAMME_CLOSED`

Two materially different mechanisms have been tested against round-level controls across the registered ten-pair Dukascopy FX Cache:

1. **Post-crossing continuation:** failed across all ten pairs; 0/10 positive-and-stable pairs.
2. **Destination and exhaustion:** failed all three preregistered primary tests.

Destination/exhaustion evidence over 2015–2021:

- 272,065 independent H1 directional-change leg endpoints showed no canonical terminal clustering;
- 47,571 causal trend approaches showed no superior canonical destination completion;
- 10,901 successful arrivals showed no stable excess reversal before equal-sized extension;
- every combined 95% interval spanned zero;
- every Holm-adjusted primary p-value was 1.0;
- independent assurance passed after rechecking all 70 annual BID/ASK source partitions and recomputing the decision;
- 2022–2025 remained unopened.

Binding decision:

`REJECT_DESTINATION_EXHAUSTION_MECHANISM`

No price-only Quarter strategy, Pine indicator, alert or deployment work is authorized. A future Quarter-related programme requires genuinely new causal information rather than further grid-threshold subdivision.
