# AS-WP-20260726-18 — Asian Sweep Extended-Reversal Model

Date opened: 2026-07-26  
Branch: `agent/asia-sweep-extended-reversal`  
State: `ACTIVE_PREREGISTERED`

## Objective

Develop target-specific causal models for reversal moves that extend materially beyond the Asian midpoint, then conditionally authorize the frozen 50%, 25% and no-TP1 position structures.

## Scope

- build 2R, 3R, 4R, opposite-boundary and opposing-liquidity labels from actual landmark entries;
- train separate T0–T3 target models under grouped out-of-fold evaluation;
- select a primary extended target and landmark only through frozen gates;
- open TP1-fraction comparison only after the target model passes.

## Protected boundaries

- no midpoint-success label reuse;
- no target, landmark, pair, weekday or direction rescue;
- no position-structure P&L before a target model passes;
- no 2020–2025 access;
- no continuation trades or combined strategy;
- no deployment.
