## Binding result

`PROMOTE_DAILY_WEEKLY_PIVOT_TERMINAL_ZONE_TO_HOLDOUT_CONFIRMATION`

The broad-zone follow-up was opened because the parent study's ±25%-spacing placebo centers could plausibly have occupied the same wider pivot neighborhood as the exact pivot.

The new study used a primary ±20% local-spacing pivot zone and equal-width controls centered halfway between adjacent pivots, plus a continuous 0–50% distance-gradient test.

## What survived

Only the occupancy-adjusted terminal-zone mechanism survives:

- daily S5: +4.55 pp preregistered close-occupancy effect; Holm p <0.001; 10/10 positive pairs;
- weekly S5: +1.26 pp; Holm p 0.008; 9/10 positive pairs.

Adversarial same-geometry exposure robustness using H1 highs/lows retained:

- daily +2.24 pp, 95% CI +1.95 to +2.52 pp;
- weekly +0.63 pp, 95% CI +0.35 to +0.91 pp.

Daily remains positive after removing any one pair or one named pivot family. Weekly does likewise.

## What did not survive

No broad pivot-zone target-completion, containment/stall or reversal-before-extension mechanism passed. Monthly, quarterly and yearly terminal-zone enrichment also failed.

The parent exact-coordinate null result remains valid. The follow-up refines it rather than reversing it.

## Interpretation

The evidence supports a daily/weekly **terminal hazard** effect: conditional on market occupancy, H1 highs/lows closer to a daily or weekly pivot are more likely to become the hindsight-confirmed endpoint of the ATR directional leg than H1 highs/lows farther between pivots.

It does not show that pivots attract price, guarantee reversal, or provide a live entry trigger.
