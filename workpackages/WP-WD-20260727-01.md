## Objective

Build and validate a causal direction-first framework in which macro, regime, seasonality and D1 market structure establish direction; H4 moving averages grade trend health; and monthly pivots define location and reach.

## Deliverables

1. direction-first preregistration;
2. deterministic D1 structural state machine;
3. H4 EMA21/55/200 health engine;
4. monthly pivot location and reach ledger;
5. seasonality contract and expanding-window implementation;
6. macro/regime point-in-time data qualification record;
7. six-pair 2015–2021 direction ledger;
8. conditional reach atlas with breadth and stability controls;
9. independent review, decision, roadmap, changelog and handover.

## Boundaries

- separate strategy family from DTR and Asian Sweep;
- six primary FX pairs;
- development years 2015–2021 only;
- D1 structure is mandatory for direction;
- H4 averages grade health but do not create direction;
- monthly pivots define location/reach but do not create direction;
- daily pivots are optional tactical context only;
- no entry, stop, partial-exit, runner or breakeven optimization;
- no 2022–2025 outcomes;
- no Pine, alerts, sizing, paper trading or deployment.

## Active task

Implement the causal D1 structure engine and its synthetic validation suite before opening real-data outcomes.
